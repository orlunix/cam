CAM Desktop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-24

CAM Desktop collects no personal data. The app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no accounts on our servers.

What the app does

  • Connects over SSH to machines you configure, using credentials you provide. All terminal and agent data travels only between your device and your own hosts.
  • SSH passwords you choose to save are stored only in the operating system's secure storage (Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows) and never leave your device.
  • SSH private keys are read only from file paths you explicitly select; they are never uploaded anywhere.

What we never see

  • Your hosts, credentials, keys, terminal contents, or agent output
  • Usage statistics, crash reports, or device identifiers

Third-party services

None. The app talks only to the SSH hosts and (optionally) the relay server that you configure yourself.
